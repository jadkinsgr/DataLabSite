// BUILT FOR THE STANDARDS THAT DEFINE QUALITY

One console for every measure that matters.

CMS Star Ratings, HEDIS, Leapfrog, HCAHPS, AHRQ PSI, Joint Commission, and NQF measures - mapped once on the same validated Gold layer, then surfaced live instead of rebuilt every reporting cycle.

We treat clinical insight as a system, not a stack of spreadsheets. Readmissions, mortality, patient experience, and CMS star ratings all read from the same validated Gold layer, so an improvement opportunity surfaced in one report holds up everywhere else it matters.

Unified measures

One Gold-layer definition per metric, feeding every dashboard, regulator, and payer report the same number.

Early signal detection

Risk and quality shifts surface from unified ADT, claims, and care-management data - before they land in a CMS refresh.

Closed-loop improvement

Findings route back to the teams who own the workflow, so an opportunity gets tracked to resolution, not just reported.
